Research Data Infrastructure

Patent–Firm and Inventor Matching Pipelines

Research-grade databases engineered from administrative and archival sources for the study of M&A and innovation. The pipelines fuzzy-match roughly 1 million patents, 46 million citation records, and 5.1 million trademark owners to firms, and construct inventor-year panels covering 150,000+ inventors at acquired technology companies. Built in Python and R with TF-IDF–based entity matching, DuckDB, and Parquet.

Measuring Product Innovation from Trademark Filings

A method for extracting new product launches from USPTO trademark filings, enabling commercialization to be measured separately from patent-based invention. Used in my dissertation to show that private equity and corporate acquirers reshape different margins of target innovation.

Shareholder Proposal Database

A hand-classified database of 1,800+ shareholder proposals linked to SEC filings, lobbying records, ESG ratings, and financial data, supporting research on investor activism and shareholder democracy.
