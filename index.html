About Research Projects Teaching CV

I am a Lecturer in Strategic Management at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania, where I teach courses on strategy and mergers & acquisitions at the undergraduate and executive MBA levels. I received my PhD in Managerial Science & Applied Economics from Wharton in August 2026.

My research examines how ownership shapes firm strategies. My dissertation compares private equity and corporate acquirers of technology companies — drawing on 3,000+ acquisitions and 150,000+ inventors — and shows that who buys a firm is a first-order determinant of what happens to its innovation. Additional work studies shareholder activism and corporate governance. Methodologically, I combine causal inference with large-scale data engineering on patent, trademark, and financial data.

Before my doctorate, I was a senior consultant in Cognizant’s digital strategy practice and earned an MBA with distinction from the University of Michigan’s Ross School of Business. Earlier, I spent four years at the Initiative for a Competitive Inner City, a nonprofit founded by Michael Porter of Harvard Business School, after completing my undergraduate degree at the Kenan-Flagler Business School at UNC Chapel Hill.
